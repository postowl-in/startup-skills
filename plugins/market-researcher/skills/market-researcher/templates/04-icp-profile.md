> who use Figma daily but manage 3–8 active client projects without a PM."

## 8. ICP VALIDATION

### How to Find Real Versions of This Person
- [ ] Post in [specific community] looking for this type of person for a 15-min call
- [ ] Search [LinkedIn] for [title + company size + industry]
- [ ] DM [N] people who posted about [specific pain] on [platform]
- [ ] Reach out to [N] reviewers on [G2/Capterra] who complained about [specific issue]

### Problem Interview Questions (15-minute format)
1. "Walk me through the last time you had to [do the thing your product helps with]. What happened?"
2. "What tools or methods do you currently use for this? How's that working?"
3. "What's the most frustrating part of that process?"
4. "How often does this come up in your work?"
5. "If you could wave a magic wand and fix this perfectly, what would that look like?"
6. "Have you ever paid for a tool to help with this? What happened?"
7. "Is this something you're actively looking to improve, or have you accepted it as is?"

**Green light signals** (this pain is real and worth building for):
- They describe the problem without prompting
- They can recall a specific recent incident
- They've already tried to solve it (paid or free)
- They get animated/emotional describing the pain

**Red light signals** (hypothesis might be wrong):
- They say "it's fine" or "not really a big deal"
- They can't recall a recent example
- They describe a workaround that's "good enough"
- They wouldn't pay for a solution even if it existed
