## WEEK 1–2: PROBLEM VALIDATION

**Goal**: Confirm the problem exists, is frequent, and is felt acutely by your ICP.  
**Method**: Listen, don't pitch. Find people with the problem. Ask about their experience.  
**Threshold**: 5+ people describe the same pain unprompted.

### Action Plan

**Day 1–3: Research existing conversations**
- [ ] Search Reddit for [specific query] — find threads where ICP describes this pain
  - Query 1: `site:reddit.com "[pain description]"` → Found: Y/N | Best thread: [URL]
  - Query 2: `site:reddit.com "[niche] frustrated"` → Found: Y/N
  - Query 3: [Additional query]
- [ ] Mine G2/Capterra reviews for [Top 2 competitors] — look for recurring complaint pattern
  - Top pattern found: "[Quote]" — appears in [N] reviews
- [ ] Search Twitter/X for live frustration: `"[pain keyword]"` past 30 days
  - Signal found: Y/N | Best example: [URL or quote]
- [ ] Check Quora: "What tools do you use for [X]?" — gauge satisfaction with current tools
  - Signal: [What found]

**Pain validation scorecard:**
| Signal source | Pain confirmed? | Severity | Notes |
|--------------|----------------|----------|-------|
| Reddit | Y/N | 🔴/🟡/🟢 | [Top finding] |
| Review mining | Y/N | 🔴/🟡/🟢 | [Top finding] |
| Twitter | Y/N | 🔴/🟡/🟢 | [Top finding] |
| Quora | Y/N | 🔴/🟡/🟢 | [Top finding] |
| **Total** | **[N/4 confirmed]** | | |

**Day 4–7: Problem interviews (5–10 people)**

*Find interview subjects:*
- [ ] Post in [subreddit]: "[Specific non-promotional post asking about their experience with X]"
  - Post copy: "[Draft the post — research-framing, not selling]"
- [ ] DM 5 people who complained in [specific Reddit thread or review]: "[Message template below]"
- [ ] Post in [Facebook Group / Discord]: "[Request for 15-min conversation]"
- [ ] LinkedIn: search [title + keywords], DM [N] people: "[Message template]"

*DM template (problem interview request):*
```
Subject: Quick question about [pain area]

Hey [Name],

I saw your post about [specific thing they said]. I'm researching [this problem space] 
and would love to hear more about your experience — not selling anything, 
just trying to understand the problem better.

Would you be up for a 15-minute call this week? Happy to work around your schedule.

[Your name]
```

*Interview questions (15 min):*
1. "Walk me through the last time you had to [do the thing]. What happened?"
2. "What do you currently use for this?"
3. "What's the most frustrating part?"
4. "How often does this come up?"
5. "Have you paid for a tool to help with this before? What happened?"
6. "What would a perfect solution look like?" ← Save this for last

*Interview notes format:*
```

Their current situation:
[Notes]

"[Direct quote]"

[List]

Reaction when I described the concept:
[Notes — watch for: lean in vs. polite interest]

Would they pay? Evidence:
[Quote or behavior signal]

Red flags:
[Anything that challenged your hypothesis]
```

**Week 1–2 Threshold**: Did 5+ people independently describe the same pain in similar words?
- [ ] Yes → Move to Step 2
- [ ] No → Pivot: [what to change] or Kill: [reason]

## WEEK 3–4: SOLUTION DESIRABILITY + WILLINGNESS TO PAY

**Goal**: Show them a description/mockup of your solution. Get commitment (not just interest).  
**Method**: Landing page + pre-orders or waitlist with strong conversion signal.  
**Threshold**: 50 email signups OR 5 people say "I'd pay $X today."

### Landing Page (build in 1 day with no-code)

**Tool**: Carrd ($19/yr) / Framer / Notion + Super / Webflow

**Landing page structure**:
```
Headline: "[Specific outcome for specific person]"
Subheadline: "[How you deliver it, without [pain]]"
Pain section: "You know that feeling when [pain description]..."
Solution: "That's exactly what [Product] solves..."
Social proof: "[If available — beta tester quote or relevant credential]"
CTA: "[Sign up for early access / Join waitlist / Pre-order at $X]"
```

**URL**: [Your domain or [productname].carrd.co]  
**CTA type**: 
- [ ] Waitlist (email only) — low commitment, weak signal
- [ ] Pre-order at full price — strongest signal
- [ ] Pre-order at discount (lifetime deal) — strong signal, good for indie hackers
- [ ] "Request invite" with context questions — medium signal + learns more

*Note: 100 waitlist emails = ~5 paying customers. 5 pre-orders = strong signal.*

### Demand Channels (drive traffic to landing page)

**Week 3: Organic / community channels (free)**
- [ ] Post in [subreddit r/X]: "[Share problem/pain post that links to landing page at end]"
  - Post title: "[How to frame it as discussion, not ad]"

- [ ] Post in [IH/community]: "[Founder journal / "I'm building X" post]"

### Price Validation

**Method A: Tiered pricing on landing page**
Show [3 price points]. See which tier gets the most clicks/signups.
- Plan A ($[X]/mo): [N] signups
- Plan B ($[Y]/mo): [N] signups  
- Plan C ($[Z]/mo): [N] signups

**Method B: Ask in follow-up email**
After signup: "We're deciding on pricing. What would you pay for [specific value]?"
- $[X]/mo: [N] responses
- $[Y]/mo: [N] responses
- $[Z]/mo: [N] responses

**Method C: Direct ask in interview**
"If I had this ready today, what would you pay per month?"
- Lowest number you'd accept: $[X] (don't anchor them)
- Their response: "[Quote]"

**Week 3–4 Threshold**:
- [ ] 50 email signups → Promising, validate further
- [ ] 5+ pre-orders at any price → Strong signal
- [ ] 3 people say "I'd pay $X right now" → Good signal
- [ ] High traffic, near-zero signups → Demand exists, messaging problem
- [ ] Low traffic, low signups → Distribution problem, not validation

### Decision Gates

**After 2 weeks of landing page**:
- 50+ signups → Continue building
- 20–49 signups → Adjust messaging, try new channels, 2 more weeks
- Under 20 signups → Pivot or kill

**After first 10 customers**:
- All 10 using core feature weekly → Build next feature they request
- 5–9 active → Understand why others aren't using it, fix onboarding
- Under 5 active → Major product-market fit issue, interview churners

**After 90 days**:
- Monthly churn under 5% → Healthy, invest in growth
- Monthly churn 5–10% → Retention issue, fix before growth
- Monthly churn over 10% → Core product problem, fix before anything else

## PIVOT TRIGGERS

If these happen, stop and reassess before continuing:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Landing page at 500 visits, under 5 signups | Messaging pivot: rewrite headline, test new angles |
| 50 signups, 0 pre-orders | Willingness-to-pay problem: lower price or different model |
| 10 customers, 8 churned in 30 days | Product problem: emergency interviews with churners |
| Can't get interviews (people won't talk to you) | Wrong channel: try different communities |
| 5 interviews, all say pain isn't that bad | Wrong ICP or wrong pain: pivot hypothesis |
| Competitor launches exactly this while validating | Niche down faster: find segment they won't serve |
